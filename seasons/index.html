Spring

March – May

Japan's most photographed season. The cherry blossom front (*sakura zensen*) sweeps from Kyushu in March to Hokkaido in early May, turning parks, canals, and castle moats pink and white. Spring festivals, mild temperatures (8–22°C), and outdoor markets make this the most popular season of the year — book early.

Recommended prefectures in spring

🌸 Best in Spring
Kyoto Philosopher's Path & Maruyama Park in full bloom
→
🌸 Japan's Finest Sakura
Aomori Hirosaki Castle — 2,600 cherry trees lining the moat
→
🌸 Samurai Cherry Walk
Akita Kakunodate — weeping cherries along the samurai quarter
→
🌸 Urban Sakura
Tokyo Meguro River tunnel & Shinjuku Gyoen in bloom
→
🌸 Mountain Sakura
Nara Yoshino — 30,000 cherry trees cascading down a mountainside
→
🌸 Castle & Sakura
Osaka Osaka Castle garden & Kema Sakuranomiya riverside path
→

Summer

June – August

Japan's summer is a season of intense festivals, tropical beaches, and mountain escapes. Heat and humidity in the big cities (30–36°C in Tokyo and Osaka) push smart travellers toward Hokkaido, Nagano, or the Okinawa islands. Summer matsuri — Nebuta, Tanabata, Awa Odori — are Japan's largest public events.

Recommended prefectures in summer

🌿 Best Summer Escape
Hokkaido Furano lavender fields & cool alpine air — max 22°C in July
→
🌊 Japan's Best Beaches
Okinawa Turquoise water, coral reefs & island snorkelling
→
🎆 Japan's Biggest Festival
Aomori Nebuta Matsuri — giant illuminated floats, 3 million spectators
→
🎋 Tanabata Origin
Miyagi Sendai Tanabata — Japan's largest bamboo-and-paper festival
→
🏔️ Cool Highland Escapes
Nagano Karuizawa, Kamikochi & trekking in the Japanese Alps
→
🎊 Japan's Most Intense Festival
Fukuoka Hakata Gion Yamakasa — 5am float races through the city
→

🍂

Autumn

September – November

Autumn is Japan's second great travel season. Maples (*momiji*) and ginkgos illuminate temples, gardens, and mountains in red, orange, and gold from mid-October through mid-December. Like the cherry blossom front, the colour front descends from northern Hokkaido — giving flexible travellers weeks to chase it.

Recommended prefectures in autumn

🍂 Japan's #1 Autumn Leaves
Kyoto Eikan-do, Tofuku-ji & the ancient maples of Nanzen-ji
→
🍂 Japan's Earliest Koyo
Hokkaido Daisetsuzan — Japan's first autumn colours from mid-September
→
🍂 Gold Mountain Shrines
Tochigi Nikko — ornate shrines in a mountain amphitheatre of red maples
→
🍂 Deer Among the Foliage
Nara Kasuga Taisha & deer under the red maples of Yoshino
→
⛩️ Torii & Autumn Leaves
Hiroshima Miyajima — floating torii gate surrounded by crimson maples
→
♨️ Onsen & Mountain Colour
Yamagata Ginzan Onsen & Zao mountain autumn — beautifully uncrowded
→

Winter

December – February

Japan's winter is the least crowded and most affordable season — except for Hokkaido during ski season. December illuminations transform the cities; hot spring (*onsen*) bathing peaks when snow falls outside; and rural Japan — Shirakawa-go, Ginzan Onsen, Kyoto's countryside — is beautiful under snow. Okinawa stays mild (18–22°C) all winter.

Recommended prefectures in winter

❄️ World's Best Powder Snow
Hokkaido Niseko skiing & Sapporo Snow Festival — monumental ice sculptures
→
❄️ Snow-Draped Garden
Ishikawa Kenroku-en garden under snow & Kanazawa's geisha districts
→
❄️ UNESCO Snow Village
Gifu Shirakawa-go — thatched farmhouses under deep winter snow
→
🌺 Japan's Winter Escape
Okinawa 18–22°C in January, Japan's earliest cherry blossoms in February
→
⛷️ Yukiguni Snow Country
Niigata Kawabata's Nobel snow country — skiing, onsen & rice sake
→
✨ Winter Illuminations
Tokyo Christmas illuminations, Omotesando & New Year's countdown
→
