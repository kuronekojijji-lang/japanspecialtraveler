Japan Travel Itineraries

Day-by-day plans for every trip length — quick 2-day city escapes to epic 14-day grand tours, organised by region.

🌆

2-Day Itineraries

Perfect Weekend · No Compromise

Two days is enough to fall in love with Japan. These itineraries pack the essential highlights of each destination — iconic temples, local markets, and food you simply cannot find anywhere else.

Choose your destination

Kantō Region 2D
Tokyo Weekend Escape
Shinjuku → Asakusa → Shibuya → Harajuku

Senso-ji at dawn, Meiji Jingu in the morning, Shibuya Crossing at sunset. The best of Tokyo in 48 hours — without rushing.

Explore Tokyo →
Kansai Region 2D
Osaka City Break
Dotonbori → Shinsekai → Osaka Castle → Namba

Takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and kushikatsu all in one weekend. Osaka Castle in the morning, Dotonbori neon at night — Japan's most delicious city.

Explore Osaka →
Kansai Region 2D
Kyoto in 2 Days
Fushimi Inari → Gion → Arashiyama → Philosopher's Path

Fushimi Inari at dawn (before the crowds), Gion stroll in the late afternoon, and matcha in Arashiyama. Two days to touch the soul of the ancient capital.

Explore Kyoto →
Chūgoku Region 2D
Hiroshima & Miyajima
Peace Park → A-Bomb Dome → Miyajima → Floating Torii

Peace Memorial Park in the morning — profound and unmissable. The floating torii of Itsukushima in the afternoon. This duo makes one of Japan's most powerful 2-day pairings.

Explore Hiroshima →
Kyūshū Region 2D
Fukuoka Foodie Break
Dazaifu → Canal City → Nakasu Yatai → Hakata Station

Dazaifu Shrine in the morning, Hakata tonkotsu ramen at lunch, yatai street stalls on the Nakasu riverbank at night. Japan's most affordable gourmet city.

Explore Fukuoka →
Hokkaidō 2D
Sapporo Snow Escape
Odori Park → Beer Museum → Susukino → Sapporo Market

Clock Tower, Odori Park in winter, Sapporo Beer Museum and Hokkaido soup curry. In February, the Sapporo Snow Festival turns the city into an ice art gallery.

Explore Hokkaido →
JR Pass tip: For a 2-day trip in a single city, the IC Card (Suica/ICOCA) is all you need — skip the JR Pass.

3-Day Itineraries

Long Weekend · Regional Focus

Three days gives enough time to properly immerse yourself in one city or link two nearby destinations. You can see the heart of a region without feeling rushed.

Choose your region

Kantō 3D
Tokyo 3-Day Essentials
Asakusa → Harajuku → Akihabara → TeamLab → Shibuya

Day 1: Asakusa and historic Tokyo. Day 2: Harajuku, Omotesando, and Shibuya Crossing. Day 3: Akihabara and a digital art experience. Three days, three faces of Tokyo.

Explore Tokyo →
Kansai 3D
Kyoto Classical 3 Days
Arashiyama → Philosopher's Path → Gion → Fushimi Inari

Day 1: Arashiyama, bamboo grove, Tenryu-ji. Day 2: Philosopher's Path, Ginkaku-ji, Gion evening. Day 3: Fushimi Inari at dawn, then Nishiki Market. The quintessence of the ancient capital.

Explore Kyoto →
Kantō / Chūbu 3D
Hakone & Mt. Fuji
Hakone Loop → Open-Air Museum → Lake Ashi → Owakudani

Mountain cog railway, ropeway over Owakudani, Lake Ashi cruise with Fuji views. Ryokan night with private hot spring bath. The best onsen experience within 1 hour of Tokyo.

Explore Kanagawa →
Chūbu (Hokuriku) 3D
Kanazawa 3-Day City Break
Kenroku-en → Higashi Chaya → Omicho Market → 21st Century Museum

One of Japan's most beautiful gardens, an intact geisha quarter, and the freshest kaisen-don market in the country. Kanazawa is the city hurried visitors miss — and regret.

Explore Ishikawa →
Kansai 3D
Osaka & Nara 3 Days
Osaka Castle → Dotonbori → Nara Park → Todai-ji → Naramachi

Two days devouring Osaka (street food, castle, Kuromon market), one day with the deer and giant Buddhas of Nara. The perfect long-weekend combination.

Explore Nara →
Kyūshū 3D
Beppu & Yufuin Onsen Break
Beppu Hells → Sand Onsen → Yufuin Village → Lake Kinrin

Beppu's boiling "hell" springs and the nostalgic village of Yufuin with its misty lake. The sand onsen burial, unique in the world, is an experience you won't forget.

Transport tip: A 7-day JR Pass (¥50,000) pays off if you combine cities like Tokyo→Osaka over 3 days. For a single city, buy tickets individually.

5-Day Itineraries

Ideal Length · Multi-Region

Five days is the ideal length for a first trip to Japan — enough time to travel between two or three regions, adjust to the time zone, and start to understand how this country works.

Choose your route

Kantō → Kansai 5D
Mini Golden Route
Tokyo (2N) → Hakone (1N) → Kyoto (1N) → Osaka (1N)

The classic circuit in compact form. Tokyo for modernity, Hakone for onsen and Fuji views, Kyoto for temples, Osaka for food. This route summarises Japan better than any other.

Full itinerary →
Kansai 5D
Kansai Classic Loop
Osaka (2N) → Nara (day trip) → Kyoto (2N) → Osaka

Base yourself in affordable Osaka, take a day trip to Nara (30 min by train), then move to Kyoto for 2 nights. This loop lets you see three of Japan's richest cities.

Explore Osaka →
Kantō → Japanese Alps 5D
Tokyo & The Japanese Alps
Tokyo (2N) → Hakone (1N) → Matsumoto (1N) → Takayama (1N)

Leave Tokyo for the mountains. Hakone for onsen, Matsumoto Castle for history, then Takayama's preserved Edo-period town for sake and Hida beef. An off-the-beaten-path favourite.

Explore Gifu →
Kyūshū 5D
Kyūshū Highlights
Fukuoka (2N) → Nagasaki (1N) → Kumamoto (1N) → Mt. Aso (1N)

Fukuoka for Hakata ramen, Nagasaki for history, Kumamoto for the castle, and the active crater of Mt. Aso. Kyushu is overlooked by visitors — their loss.

Explore Fukuoka →
Tōhoku 5D
Tōhoku Discovery
Tokyo → Nikko (1N) → Sendai (1N) → Matsushima → Yamadera → Yamagata (1N)

From Nikko's golden shrines to Matsushima's pine-covered islands and the mountain temple of Yamadera. Japan's most authentic and least-known region.

Explore Miyagi →
7-Day JR Pass: For a 5-day multi-city loop, the 7-day JR Pass typically pays for itself if you take even one Tokyo→Osaka shinkansen.

7-Day Itineraries

One Week · Complete Circuit

One week is the sweet spot for a complete Japan circuit — enough time to explore two or three regions properly, get lost in a neighbourhood, and take a scenic train.

Choose your route

Kantō → Kansai 7D
The Golden Route Classic
Tokyo (3N) → Kamakura → Hakone (1N) → Kyoto (2N) → Nara → Osaka (1N)

Japan's most-visited circuit for a reason — it delivers the best temples, best onsen, best street food, and a Fuji view. Perfect for a first-time visit.

See full plan →
Alps → Kansai 7D
Alps & Historic Cities
Tokyo (2N) → Takayama (2N) → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa (1N) → Kyoto (1N) → Osaka

The favourite circuit for travellers who've already done the Golden Route. Edo-era Takayama, UNESCO Shirakawa-go villages, gold-leaf Kanazawa, then Kyoto.

Explore Gifu →
Hokkaidō 7D
Hokkaidō Grand Tour
Hakodate (1N) → Noboribetsu (1N) → Sapporo (2N) → Otaru → Furano/Asahikawa (2N)

The northern island at your own pace: Hakodate's squid traps, Noboribetsu's hell baths, Sapporo beer, Furano lavender (July). Hokkaido's food alone justifies the trip.

Explore Hokkaido →
Kyūshū 7D
Kyūshū Complete Loop
Fukuoka (2N) → Nagasaki (1N) → Kumamoto (1N) → Kagoshima (1N) → Beppu (1N) → Fukuoka

A complete circuit of Japan's third-largest island — castles, colonial history, active volcanoes, onsen, and ramen. Kyushu offers surprising diversity for its size.

Explore Fukuoka →
Kantō → Tōhoku 7D
Nikko & Deep Tōhoku
Tokyo (1N) → Nikko (1N) → Sendai (1N) → Matsushima → Yamadera → Aomori (2N) → Kakunodate (1N)

From Nikko's golden shrines to Matsushima's pine islands, Yamadera temple, Aomori's nebuta, and the samurai district of Kakunodate. One week beyond Japan's tourist trail.

Explore Aomori →
Seasonal tip: For a 7-day trip, seasonality matters enormously. Cherry blossoms (Mar–Apr) and autumn foliage (Nov) are spread across the country — check regional forecasts before finalising your route.

10-Day Itineraries

In Depth · Multi-Region

Ten days lets you step off the Golden Route and explore less-visited regions without feeling rushed. This is the length where Japan starts to really make sense — the rhythms, the flavours, the respect for detail.

Choose your route

Kantō → Chūgoku 10D
Grand Golden Route
Tokyo (3N) → Kamakura → Hakone (1N) → Kyoto (3N) → Nara → Osaka (1N) → Hiroshima (1N) → Miyajima

The extended classic — with Kamakura, a Hakone overnight, a full day in Nara, and a Hiroshima-Miyajima side trip. The most complete circuit for a first long trip.

Tōhoku → Hokkaidō 10D
Tōhoku & Hokkaidō
Tokyo (1N) → Sendai (1N) → Matsushima → Aomori (2N) → Hakodate (1N) → Noboribetsu (1N) → Sapporo (3N)

From Aomori's nebuta festival to Sapporo's sea urchin miso soup, via Matsushima's pine islands. The often-ignored Japanese North is also its most authentic.

Mountain Japan Route
Tokyo (2N) → Nikko (1N) → Nagano (1N) → Matsumoto (1N) → Takayama (2N) → Kanazawa (1N) → Kyoto (1N) → Osaka

From Nikko's shrines to the Alpine medieval towns. This circuit follows Japan's mountain spine without doing the same thing twice.

Kansai 10D
Deep Kansai Immersion
Osaka (2N) → Kyoto (3N) → Nara (1N) → Koyasan (1N) → Yoshino (1N) → Ise (1N) → Nagoya (1N)

Japan's culturally richest region explored in depth. Koyasan for temple sleeping, Yoshino for cherry blossoms, and Ise Jingu for spiritual Japan.

Japan Food Pilgrimage
Tokyo → Kanazawa → Kyoto → Osaka → Fukuoka → Kagoshima

An itinerary organised entirely around Japan's finest food — sushi in Tokyo, kaisendon in Kanazawa, kaiseki in Kyoto, street food in Osaka, ramen in Fukuoka, wagyu in Kagoshima.

14-Day Itineraries

Grand Tour · North to South

Two weeks in Japan lets you truly get lost — change pace, accept an unplanned detour, and step completely off the tourist trail. These itineraries are for travellers who want more than the minimum.

Choose your grand circuit

Spring · National 14D
Cherry Blossom Season Route
Tokyo → Hakone → Takayama → Kanazawa → Kyoto → Nara → Osaka → Hiroshima

Japan's most popular 14-day circuit, timed for cherry blossoms — late March to mid-April. From Tokyo's parks to Yoshino's forest, following the bloom front westward.

See full plan →
Autumn · National 14D
Autumn Foliage Route
Hokkaido → Tohoku → Nikko → Kyoto → Nara → Osaka → Hiroshima

Follow autumn colours from north to south — Hokkaido in red and gold in October, Kyoto in deep crimson in November. The country's most visually spectacular itinerary.

See full plan →
Tokyo → Kyūshū 14D
The Ultimate Japan Grand Tour
Tokyo (3N) → Takayama (2N) → Kanazawa (1N) → Kyoto (3N) → Osaka (1N) → Hiroshima (1N) → Fukuoka (2N) → Kagoshima (1N)

From Tokyo to Kagoshima's volcanoes, via the Japanese Alps, Kanazawa, Kyoto, and Hiroshima. The most complete circuit possible in 14 days, north to south.

Full itinerary →
Tōhoku → Hokkaidō 14D
Tōhoku & Hokkaidō Grand Tour
Tokyo (2N) → Nikko (1N) → Sendai (1N) → Yamagata (1N) → Aomori (2N) → Hakodate (1N) → Noboribetsu (1N) → Sapporo (3N) → Otaru

Two weeks in Northern Japan — the least touristy, most authentic part of the country. Nikko pagodas in forest, Aomori nebuta festivals, Hokkaido landscapes, and Hakodate dawn seafood.

Off the Beaten Path
Tottori → Shimane → Yamaguchi → Shikoku → Kochi → Ehime → Matsuyama
