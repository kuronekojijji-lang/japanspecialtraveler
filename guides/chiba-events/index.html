Chiba is where most visitors land (Narita Airport) and where Tokyo Disney Resort actually sits — but the prefecture’s own 2026 event calendar is far richer than a transit stop suggests. It hosts some of the Kanto region’s biggest summer fireworks, two UNESCO-linked Gion float festivals on the same weekend, the world’s largest video-game show, a temple-steps display of thirty thousand hina dolls, and whale-and-dolphin watching off its Pacific coast. This guide gathers the events worth planning a 2026 trip around, with a month-by-month calendar at the end.

A note on dates: Confirmed 2026 dates are given where announced. Festival and firework dates can shift for weather or organiser decisions, and ticketed events sell out — always confirm on the official website before building a day around one.

Summer Fireworks (Hanabi) — 2026 Dates

Chiba packs several of the Kanto region’s largest displays into a few weeks of high summer.

Fireworks display 2026 date Notes
Makuhari Beach Fireworks Festa (幕張ビーチ花火フェスタ), 48th August 1 (Sat) ~24,000 shells over Makuhari’s seafront — Chiba’s biggest, marking the 900th anniversary of Chiba’s founding
Teganuma Fireworks (手賀沼花火大会) August 1 (Sat) ~10,000 shells over Lake Teganuma (Kashiwa & Abiko)
Choshi Minato Fireworks (銚子みなとまつり花火大会) August 8 (Sat) ~8,000 shells at the fishing port
Kisarazu Port Festival Fireworks (木更津港まつり) August 15 (Sat), 19:05–20:30 ~14,000 shells over Kisarazu’s inner harbour — a hugely popular Tokyo Bay display

Tip: Makuhari and Teganuma fall on the same night — pick one. The Kisarazu display on Aug 15 is a favourite for its bay setting and views back toward the Tokyo skyline; go early for a beach or park spot.

Tokyo Game Show 2026 (東京ゲームショウ) — Sept 17–21, Makuhari Messe ⭐

One of the world’s largest video-game expos, held at Makuhari Messe in Chiba City. 2026 marks the 30th anniversary, running an unprecedented five days: September 17–18 are business days and September 19–21 (Sat–Mon holiday) are open to the public. Expect enormous crowds, playable pre-release titles, cosplay, esports stages, and international studios. Public-day tickets are timed and sell out — buy in advance, and go early to manage the queues.

Traditional Gion Festivals — July 10–12, 2026

In a rare alignment, two of Chiba’s great float festivals fall on the same weekend of July 10–12 in 2026 (the first time in 11 years):

Narita Gion Festival (成田祇園祭)

Three days of ornate wooden floats and mikoshi hauled through the streets around Naritasan Shinshoji temple and its approach, to drums and flutes — a spirited, accessible festival minutes from Narita Airport.

Sawara no Taisai (佐原の大祭) — summer festival

Held in the beautifully preserved canal town of Sawara (Katori), this UNESCO-inscribed float festival parades enormous floats topped with towering figures through Edo-era streets. There is also an autumn edition (usually mid-October). One of the Kanto region’s three great float festivals.

Katsuura Big Hina Matsuri (かつうらビッグひな祭り) — Feb 21 – Mar 3, 2026

A spectacular take on the Doll Festival: around 30,000 hina dolls are displayed across the town of Katsuura, most famously cascading down the 60 stone steps of Tomisaki Shrine. 2026 dates are February 21 – March 3, roughly 9:00–19:00. The evening illumination of the shrine-step display is unforgettable — one of Japan’s most photogenic late-winter events.

Tokyo Disney Resort (Urayasu) — Year-Round Seasonal Events

Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea, both in Urayasu, Chiba, run a rotating calendar of seasonal events through 2026 — spring/Easter, summer, Halloween (roughly September–October), and Christmas (roughly November–December), each with special parades, décor, and merchandise. Buy dated tickets in advance via the official app; the parks are busiest on weekends, holidays, and during the Halloween and Christmas seasons.

Cherry Blossom Season (Spring 2026)

Chiba’s blossoms peak in late March to early April.

  • Isumi Railway — local trains running through canola-flower (nanohana) and cherry-lined countryside, a classic springtime image
  • Mother Farm (Futtsu) — flowers and farm animals with Tokyo Bay views
  • Sakura & castle spots — the grounds around the reconstructed keeps and parks citywide

Whale & Dolphin Watching (Choshi & the Pacific coast)

Off Choshi, at the eastern tip of Chiba, boat tours run for whales and dolphins across a long season — dolphins are commonly sighted much of the year, with certain whale species more likely in the colder months (roughly autumn into winter). A distinctive Chiba experience combined with the dramatic Inubosaki cape and lighthouse.

Winter Illuminations & New Year

  • Tokyo German Village (Sodegaura) — one of the Kanto region’s largest illuminations, running through the winter season into early 2026
  • Naritasan Setsubun-e (Feb 3) — a famous bean-throwing ceremony at Naritasan Shinshoji, often with celebrity and sumo guests
  • Hatsumode at Naritasan Shinshoji — among Japan’s busiest, with around three million New Year visitors

Practical Tips

  • Book early for August fireworks and Tokyo Game Show week. Bay-area and Makuhari hotels fill fast around Aug 1/15 and the mid-September TGS.
  • Confirm every date on the official site. Firework and festival dates move for weather; ticketed events (TGS public days, Disney) sell out.
  • Chiba is easy from Tokyo and the airport. Makuhari, Narita, and the bay are all short train rides — many events work as day trips.
  • Summer heat. July–August events are hot and humid; water, a hat, and a fan are essentials.
  • Two festivals, one weekend. With Narita and Sawara on the same July dates in 2026, pick one per day or split the weekend between them.
