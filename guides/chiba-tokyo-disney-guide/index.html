Tokyo Disney Resort Guide: Tickets, Parks & Access

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Despite the name, Tokyo Disney Resort sits in Urayasu, Chiba, on the shore of Tokyo Bay — and it’s one of the world’s most popular theme-park destinations. The resort pairs two distinct parks: Tokyo Disneyland, the classic castle park beloved for its parades and fairy-tale lands, and Tokyo DisneySea, a uniquely Japanese, nautically themed park (found nowhere else) celebrated for its immersive harbours, detailed theming and more grown-up attractions. With resort hotels, the Ikspiari mall and the Disney Resort monorail tying it together, plus famously polished service and seasonal events, it’s an easy, dazzling day (or several) right next to Tokyo.

This guide covers the two parks, tickets, the best time, getting around and access.

🗓️ Quick Reference
Location Maihama, Urayasu, Chiba (Tokyo Bay)
Parks Tokyo Disneyland & Tokyo DisneySea
Time needed 1 full day per park
Best time Weekdays; avoid holidays for shorter lines
Good for Families, couples, theme-park fans

The two parks

  • Tokyo Disneyland: the classic castle park with parades, fairy-tale lands and family rides.
  • Tokyo DisneySea: a one-of-a-kind, sea-themed park with immersive harbours and more thrilling, adult-friendly attractions — a fan favourite.
  • Each park easily fills a full day; many visitors do one park per day.

Tickets & getting around

  • Buy dated tickets in advance online — parks can sell out on busy days.
  • Use the official app for waiting times, mobile ordering and ride passes.
  • The Disney Resort Line monorail loops between the parks, hotels and Maihama Station.

Best time & tips

  • Weekdays and off-peak seasons mean shorter queues; Japanese holidays are extremely busy.
  • Seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas) bring special parades and decor.
  • Arrive at opening to maximise popular rides.

Combine your visit

Easy to pair with bayfront Chiba:

  • Catch a baseball game or sea breeze at the ZOZO Marine Stadium bayfront — see our ZOZO Marine guide .
  • Attend a show or expo at Makuhari Messe — see our Makuhari Messe guide .

How to get there

  • JR Keiyo line to Maihama Station (~15 min from Tokyo Station), steps from the resort.
  • Direct buses also run from major Tokyo hubs and both airports.
