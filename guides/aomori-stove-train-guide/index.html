Tsugaru Stove Train: Aomori's Retro Winter Ride — Guide

Last updated: July 4, 2026

The Tsugaru Railway ‘stove train’ is a nostalgic winter ride through the snowy Tsugaru plain. Vintage railcars are warmed by pot-belly coal stoves, and attendants grill dried squid (surume) right on top — you buy one and eat it as the train rattles through white countryside.

🗓️ At a Glance
Location Goshogawara, Aomori
Hours Runs ~Dec–Mar, several daily
Admission Fare + stove-car supplement (~¥500)
Time needed About 1 hour each way
Best time Winter (Dec–Mar)

What to Expect

  • Vintage railcars heated by coal-burning stoves.
  • Attendants grilling dried squid (surume) on the stove.
  • Snowy Tsugaru plain scenery out the window.
  • A retro Showa-era rail atmosphere.

Combine Your Visit

  • Ride out to photograph Takayama Inari Shrine in the snow.
  • Warm up further at seaside Furofushi Onsen .
  • Combine with Hirosaki Castle in the region.

Getting There

  • By train: Tsugaru Railway from Tsugaru-Goshogawara Station.
  • Access Goshogawara: JR from Aomori/Hirosaki, then transfer.

Tips

The stove car runs only in winter (roughly December–March). Buy dried squid onboard and let the attendant grill it on the stove — that’s the experience.
