Sapporo Guide: Odori Park, Susukino & Things to Do

Sapporo, Hokkaido’s vibrant capital and Japan’s fifth-largest city, is the gateway to the north and a destination in its own right. Laid out on an easy American-style grid, it centres on the long green ribbon of Odori Park, runs north to the historic Clock Tower and former government buildings, and south into the neon buzz of Susukino, one of Japan’s great nightlife and dining districts. Add legendary food — miso ramen, soup curry, jingisukan and crab — plus year-round festivals, and Sapporo is the perfect base for exploring Hokkaido.

This guide covers the main sights, neighbourhoods, seasonal events, food and how to get around.

🗓️ Quick Reference
Location Central Hokkaido (the island’s capital)
Highlights Odori Park, TV Tower, Susukino, Clock Tower
Time needed 1–2 days
Best time Year-round (Snow Festival in Feb)
Good for City sights, food, nightlife, festivals

Odori Park & central sights

  • Odori Park: a 1.5 km green strip slicing through downtown, with fountains, flowers and the Sapporo TV Tower at its eastern end — the city’s heart and main festival venue.
  • Sapporo Clock Tower: a beloved wooden landmark from the pioneer era.
  • Former Hokkaido Government Office (“Red Brick”): a handsome neo-baroque building and gardens.
  • Mount Moiwa ropeway for a sweeping night view over the city.

Susukino & nightlife

  • Susukino: just south of Odori, one of Japan’s biggest entertainment quarters — neon streets packed with izakaya, bars, ramen and the famous Ramen Alley.
  • Great for late-night eats and a lively, safe night out.

Food & seasonal events

  • Food: Sapporo invented miso ramen and soup curry, and is a hub for jingisukan, crab and dairy — see our Sapporo ramen guide and soup curry guide .
  • Sapporo Snow Festival (Feb): giant snow sculptures fill Odori Park — see our Snow Festival guide .
  • Yosakoi Soran (Jun) and the Autumn Fest (Sep) also centre on Odori — see our Yosakoi Soran guide and autumn food festival guide .
  • Sapporo Beer Garden in summer — see our Sapporo beer guide .

Combine your visit

Sapporo is the ideal base for nearby day trips:

  • Otaru canal town is ~30 min by train — see our Otaru guide .
  • Noboribetsu hot springs and the Yoichi whisky distillery are easy outings — see our Noboribetsu guide and Nikka whisky guide .

How to get there

  • New Chitose Airport → Sapporo Station by JR rapid train (~40 min).
  • Central sights are walkable; the subway and a downtown streetcar fill in the rest.
