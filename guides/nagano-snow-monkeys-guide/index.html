Jigokudani Snow Monkey Park: Wild Macaques Bathing in Hot Springs — Guide

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Jigokudani Snow Monkey Park is famous worldwide as the only place where wild Japanese macaques (snow monkeys) regularly bathe in natural hot springs, soaking in a steaming pool to keep warm, most iconically surrounded by deep snow in winter. Reached by a roughly 30-minute walk along a forest trail from the car park, the park lets visitors observe the wild troop up close as they relax, groom and play around the pool, an experience possible year-round but at its most magical and photogenic when snow blankets the valley.

🗓️ At a Glance
Location Yamanouchi, Nagano
Hours ~9:00–16:00 (winter) / 8:30–17:00 (summer)
Admission Around ¥800
Time needed 2–3 hours including the walk
Best time Winter (Dec–Mar) for snow-surrounded bathing

What to See

  • Wild Japanese macaques bathing in a natural hot spring.
  • The troop relaxing, grooming and playing around the pool.
  • The iconic snow-surrounded scene at its best in winter.
  • A roughly 30-minute forest trail walk to reach the park.

Combine Your Visit

  • Combine with Shiga Kogen Skiing nearby.
  • Pair with Obuse chestnut town en route.
  • Read our Nagano overview .

Getting There

  • By train + bus: Nagano Station to Yudanaka by Nagaden, then a bus to the trailhead.
  • By car: parking at the trailhead, then a roughly 30-minute forest walk to the park.

Tips

The park is reached only by a roughly 30-minute walk along a forest trail, which can be snowy and slippery in winter, so wear proper waterproof footwear with good grip. Winter is the most iconic time for the snow-surrounded scene, but the monkeys are present year-round; note that on very warm days they may bathe less, so cold, snowy days actually offer the classic view.
