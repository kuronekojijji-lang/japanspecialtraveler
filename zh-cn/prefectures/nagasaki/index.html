长崎县旅游指南

长崎异国文化融合、军舰岛世界遗产、长崎灯笼节日本最大中国新年——长崎是日本最具异域风情的城市

⚓ 军舰岛（端岛）——海上废墟·世界遗产🏮 长崎灯笼节——日本最大规模的中国新年庆典🌉 眼镜桥——日本最古老的石造拱桥🌸 豪斯登堡——荷兰风格大型主题公园☮️ 长崎和平公园——原爆纪念与祈念

🗾 关于 长崎县

长崎是日本锁国时代唯一对外开放的港口，因此积累了独一无二的多元文化遗产。荷兰人、中国人、葡萄牙人在此留下了深刻印记——グラバー园的洋馆群、眼镜桥、新地中华街的长崎灯笼节，以及和洋中融合的卓袱（ちゃんこ）料理，构成了日本最独特的文化混合体。军舰岛（端岛）是19世纪至1970年代的海上煤矿城市，荒废后犹如末日废墟，现已列为世界遗产，乘船参访是日本最有震撼力的历史体验之一。长崎灯笼节（旧历正月）是日本最大规模的中国新年庆典，全城装饰2万个红灯笼，是长崎最热闹的季节。

✈️ 如何前往

从博多乘西九州新干线约70分（¥3,430）至长崎。市内路面电车约¥200覆盖主要观光点。军舰岛观光船从长崎港出发约1小时，需提前预约（约¥4,500—¥5,000）。

✈️ 从东京出发（飞机）
  • ANA / JAL 从羽田机场直飞，飞行时间约1—2.5小时，票价¥10,000—¥35,000。Peach / Jetstar等廉价航空从成田出发，价格更低，行李需另付费。
  • 提前2—4周购票可享受大幅折扣；黄金周与暑假期间票价较高，建议提前预订。
🚄 从东京出发（新干线）
  • 西九州新干线（长崎→武雄温泉约23分+在来线接续→博多约70分，¥3,430）
  • 持JR通票（JR Pass）可免费搭乘新干线，多城市旅行性价比极高；请提前预约指定席座位。
✈️ 从其他城市出发
  • 从大阪/关西出发可乘坐新干线或飞机；从福冈、名古屋等地亦有便捷的新干线或直飞服务。
  • 从香港、台北、首尔等亚洲城市均有直飞日本各主要机场的国际航班，飞行时间约2—4小时。
🚗 当地交通
  • JR长崎本线·西九州新干线（西九州特急约30分至博多）·路面电车
  • 郊外及自然景区强烈建议租车探索；持国际驾驶执照（IDP）可在日本驾车，请出发前办理。
💡 旅游小贴士长崎灯笼节（旧历1月15日前后，通常在2月）是长崎最独特的节日，此时新地中华街·唐人屋敷等地装饰璀璨，对华人游客而言是别具意义的异乡新年体验。

观光

17 景点
⛩️
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Atomic Bomb Museum

Japan's most comprehensive museum documenting the atomic bombing, with extensive artifacts, photographs, and survivor testimonies. A deeply educational and moving experience that provides crucial historical context for understanding modern peace efforts.

War History Museum Educational Moving
⛩️
📍 Port Area, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Harbor Night View

One of East Asia's most spectacular nighttime cityscapes, with harbor lights twinkling against the dark water and surrounding hills. Best enjoyed from observation decks or harbor-side restaurants for an unforgettable romantic experience.

Night Lights Harbor Romantic Photography
⛩️
📍 Inasa, Nagasaki

Inasa Observation Deck

Accessible via scenic cable car, this mountain-top platform offers 360-degree views of Nagasaki City, harbor, and surrounding landscape. Perfect for sunset viewing and one of Japan's top three night views, with a rooftop restaurant for evening dining.

Panoramic Views Cable Car Viewpoint Sunset
⛩️
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Peace Park

This solemn memorial park honors the victims of the 1945 atomic bombing with iconic sculptures and reflective spaces. The central Peace Statue overlooks the city and offers profound insight into Nagasaki's resilience and commitment to peace.

War History Memorial Monument Peaceful Grounds
⛩️
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Urakami Cathedral

This stunning neo-Romanesque cathedral was completely destroyed in the atomic bombing yet meticulously reconstructed, symbolizing Nagasaki's remarkable recovery. The beautiful red-brick structure and peaceful atmosphere make it a moving spiritual destination.

Historic Church Religious Site Architecture Resilience

📍 Minami Yamate, Nagasaki

Glover Garden

Explore beautifully preserved Western merchant mansions from the Meiji era set among lush gardens overlooking Nagasaki Harbor. This unique site captures the city's fascinating fusion of Japanese and European influences during the Dutch trading period.

Colonial Architecture Historic Mansions Garden UNESCO

Castella Cake Shop Tour

Visit traditional castella shops producing this sponge cake that arrived with Portuguese traders centuries ago and became a beloved Japanese delicacy. Watch the production process and sample fresh-baked slices paired with local tea.

Portuguese Heritage Sweets Souvenir Historic Recipe
🍜
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Castella Cake

Nagasaki's signature confection is castella — a honey-glazed sponge cake introduced by Portuguese traders in the 16th century. Fukusaya, founded in 1624 and the oldest castella shop in existence, sells wooden-boxed cakes in its original Meiji-era shopfront. The unique sugar-crystal bottom layer (zarame) distinguishes authentic Nagasaki castella from all imitations.

Castella Portuguese Sweets Souvenir
🍜
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Champon Yokocho (Noodle Alley)

Sample authentic Nagasaki champon—a unique noodle dish with pork broth and fresh seafood—in this atmospheric alley lined with family-run restaurants since the 1950s. Each stall has its own recipe, making this a delicious culinary pilgrimage.

Local Cuisine Street Food Traditional Restaurant Authentic
🍜
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Kakuni Pork Belly Specialty

Savor kakuni, Nagasaki's tender slow-cooked pork belly dish served in rich miso-based sauce—a comfort food deeply rooted in local culinary tradition. Often paired with sake or served with rice at upscale izakaya and traditional restaurants.

Local Delicacy Slow-Cooked Pork Umami Traditional
🍜
📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Sara Udon Dining

Experience sara udon, Nagasaki's signature crispy fried noodle dish served with thick savory sauce and fresh vegetables—a delightful blend of Chinese and Japanese culinary traditions. Best enjoyed at historic restaurants that have perfected the recipe for decades.

Local Noodles Crispy Noodles Chinese-Japanese Fusion Must-Try

📍 Central Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Turkey Rice Dining Experience

Sample turkey rice, a beloved Nagasaki dish combining Portuguese-inspired preparation with local ingredients—a unique fusion that originated during the Portuguese trading period. Served at specialty restaurants with traditional recipes passed through generations.

Local Specialty Portuguese Influence Rice Dish Unique Flavor

节庆

4 景点
🎆
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Peace Memorial Ceremony

Every August 9th at 11:02 a.m. — the exact moment the second atomic bomb fell in 1945 — Nagasaki holds its Peace Memorial Ceremony at the Nagasaki Peace Park. A minute's silence, bell ringing, and the mayor's Peace Declaration draw dignitaries from around the world in a solemn tribute and call for nuclear abolition.

Peace History Atomic Bomb August 9
🎆
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Lantern Festival

During the two weeks of Lunar New Year, central Nagasaki transforms as 15,000 red and gold lanterns illuminate Chinatown, Nagasaki Park, and the harbour. Giant dragon, zodiac animal, and deity figures glow against the night sky, while Chinatown fills with steaming dumplings, lion dances, and traditional Chinese street performers.

Chinese Lanterns Lunar New Year Chinatown Illumination
🎆
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Kunchi Festival (UNESCO)

Listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Nagasaki Kunchi in October is one of Japan's most exotic festivals — a reflection of the city's centuries of Chinese, Dutch, and Portuguese influences. Dragon boat dances, Chinese-style parade floats with mechanical figures, and Dutch ship replicas take turns performing in Suwa Shrine's precincts.

UNESCO Dragon Dance Festival Autumn
🎆
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Nagasaki Port Festival

Each August, Nagasaki's magnificent harbour hosts the Nagasaki Port Festival with spectacular fireworks reflecting off the enclosed bay, dragon boat racing, and a waterfront carnival. The natural amphitheatre of surrounding hills creates one of Japan's most visually dramatic fireworks settings, best viewed from the hillside observatory.

Port Festival Fireworks Harbour Summer

体验

1 景点
🎌
📍 Nagasaki City, Nagasaki

Glover Garden & Meiji-Era Life Experience

Glover Garden on Nagasaki's hillside preserves the Western-style residences of the foreign merchants who shaped Meiji Japan's modernisation. Guided walking tours explore Thomas Glover's 1863 Scottish-Japanese home — the oldest Western-style building in Japan — reconstructing daily life in the era when Nagasaki was Japan's only window to the outside world and the model for Puccini's Madama Butterfly.

Meiji Era Western Architecture History Walking Tour

💡 实用旅游信息

出发前及旅途中所需了解的一切。

🌸
最佳旅游季节
  • 长崎灯笼节（旧历1月15日前后）：全城2万个灯笼点亮，祭典游行·舞龙舞狮热闹非凡，是日本最大的中国风祭典。
  • 夏季（8月）：精灵流し（お盆放灯笼）是长崎独特的追悼仪式，15日夜晚彩灯漂流海面，美丽而感人。
  • 全年（军舰岛）：夏季天气较好、海面平稳，军舰岛观光船风险较低；冬季风浪大，登岛可能取消。
💴
旅行预算参考
  • 长崎市内商务酒店¥8,000—¥15,000/晚；豪斯登堡酒店¥20,000—¥60,000
  • 軍艦島观光船约¥4,500；豪斯登堡入园约¥7,000；グラバー园¥620
  • 長崎ちゃんぽん约¥900—¥1,500；卓袱料理（宴席）约¥10,000—¥20,000/人
🍽️
长崎美食推荐
  • 長崎ちゃんぽん：以鸡骨·猪骨高汤加入海鲜·蔬菜·面条的浓郁混炖面，是长崎最具代表性的食物，与博多拉面截然不同
  • 皿乌龙（皿うどん）：将ちゃんぽん的面换成细炸面，厚芡汁覆盖其上，是长崎独有的变形版
  • 卓袱料理（卓袱）：和·洋·中融合的宴席料理，起源于江户时代的出岛，是长崎最独特的美食文化体验
  • カステラ（蜂蜜蛋糕）：由葡萄牙传入的西洋甜点，在长崎发展出柔软细腻的独特风格，「福砂屋」「文明堂」是百年老铺
💡
